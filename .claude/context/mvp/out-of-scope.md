# MVP Out-of-Scope Features

**Purpose:** Features to DEFER to v2+ (post-MVP validation).

---

## ❌ Leagues & Tournaments
**Why defer:** Too complex for MVP, requires scheduling algorithms, bracket management

## ❌ Video Highlights
**Why defer:** Storage costs, encoding complexity, not core to match-making

## ❌ Marketplace
**Why defer:** Not core functionality, requires payment processing

## ❌ Social Feed / Following
**Why defer:** Nice-to-have, not essential for match challenges

## ❌ Push Notifications
**Why defer:** Use email notifications instead, avoid infrastructure costs

## ❌ Advanced Analytics
**Why defer:** Basic stats sufficient for MVP, advanced metrics later

## ❌ Payment Processing
**Why defer:** Handle manually initially, integrate when revenue proven

## ❌ Mobile Apps (Native)
**Why defer:** Web-first for faster demos, no app store approval delays

## ❌ Live Scoring
**Why defer:** Too complex, submit final results only

## ❌ Chat / Messaging
**Why defer:** Use email/external messaging, avoid real-time infrastructure

## ❌ Referee Management
**Why defer:** Manual for MVP, automate when scaled

---

## Strategic Decisions

- **Single sport first:** Focus on Cricket OR Football (not both initially)
- **Web before mobile:** Faster demos, no app store approval delays
- **Manual admin:** Handle disputes/moderation manually
- **Email notifications only:** Avoid push notification infrastructure
- **Local storage:** Use filesystem before paid cloud storage
- **Simple UI:** Functional over fancy (Tailwind components)

---

**Principle:** Ship working core features, defer everything else.
